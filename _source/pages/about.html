About us

Bluechip Experience Limited is a Nigerian operating and investment company based in Lagos. We build businesses, invest in opportunity and run what we own for the long term.

Where we started

Bluechip Experience Limited was incorporated on %%INCORP%%. It began in enterprise services, running workforce, outsourcing and managed services for a multinational consumer-electronics brand in Nigeria. That relationship continues, and that side of the company is now our Enterprise Services division.

Running services for a demanding client taught us how to operate. We now use the same discipline to build and back businesses of our own. That work sits in BlueX, our venture platform.
