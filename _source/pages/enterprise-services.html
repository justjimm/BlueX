Bluechip Enterprise Services

Workforce, outsourcing and managed services for companies operating in Nigeria. This is where Bluechip started, and it remains a core part of what we do.

What we do

One point of accountability for work that has to get done properly, every day.

Workforce solutions
We recruit, deploy and manage staff for client operations, and handle the administration that comes with them.
Outsourcing
We take over defined functions and run them to an agreed standard, so the client's own team can focus elsewhere.
Managed services
We run a service end to end, with regular reporting the client can check.
Operational support
Day-to-day corporate support that keeps operations moving.
Enterprise partnerships
Long-term arrangements with companies building or expanding their operations in Nigeria.

How an engagement runs

Every engagement follows the same order.

  1. Scope

    We agree what will be done, to what standard, and how it will be measured.

  2. Staff

    We assign the people and, where needed, recruit them.

  3. Run

    We run the work and deal with problems as they come up.

  4. Report

    We report against the agreed measures on a fixed schedule.

Our longest relationship
