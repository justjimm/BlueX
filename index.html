An operating company first.

Bluechip started by running things. Our first business was enterprise services: workforce, outsourcing and managed services for a multinational consumer-electronics brand in Nigeria. That work continues.

Running a service for a demanding client shows you what an opportunity needs before it can become a business: clear ownership, proper documents, the right partners, and people on the ground who run it every day. That is what we bring to each business we build or back.

BlueX

The venture platform of Bluechip.

BlueX finds commercial opportunities that are real but loosely run, and adds what they lack: capital, documents, partners, technology and people who can operate. Then it builds them into businesses that can grow.

How we work

The same four steps for every business we build or back.

  1. Find

    We look for demand that already exists and is badly served.

  2. Structure

    We settle ownership, documents and capital before we build.

  3. Build

    We put in the team, systems and partners the business needs.

  4. Operate

    We run it, report on it and stay for the long term.
